A `surface-raised` card with a media area, a `kicker` meta line in `accent-text`, an `h3` title, a `body-sm` summary and a `sv-link` call. The stacked form (`sv-card`) suits two- or three-up grids of achievements; `sv-card--row` suits lists such as courses and events.

The consumer provides media (photograph or the placeholder variants `sv-media--gold`, `sv-media--soft`), meta, title, summary (two lines) and the link text («بیشتر بخوانید» or «ثبت‌نام»). Keep one `sv-media--gold` card per row at most, as the lead item. Cards are links as a whole; do not add inner links.
