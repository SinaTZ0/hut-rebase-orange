Opens every homepage section: a `kicker` (gold rule + `brand-text`), the title in `h2`, an optional `body` lead in `ink-muted`, and an optional `outline` button at the opposite end for «مشاهده همه». On `surface-deep` the kicker turns `accent` and the lead `on-deep-muted` automatically.

The consumer provides the kicker (three words at most), the title and the lead (one sentence). The rule is a decoration and sits at the inline-start edge; do not hand-draw it. Use `h1` only for the page's own title.
