An inline notice with a one-line title (`h4`, in the tone colour) and a `body-sm` sentence. The tone sets the border, icon and title; the body text stays `ink`. Use `role="alert"` for warning and danger, `role="status"` for the rest.

The consumer provides the title, the message, and the matching icon: `info`, `check-circle-2`, `alert-triangle`, `circle-alert`. State what happened and what to do next. Do not stack more than three notices; put official announcements in the news list instead.
